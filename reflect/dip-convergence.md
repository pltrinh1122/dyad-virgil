## Slot derivations

- **`craft` — relational-model mismatch, not relationship-type.** The Operator arrived with a
  prior discovery riff and its own falsification already run: the starting hypothesis (relating
  has two facets, with-self and with-others, one fundamental across different "others") was
  **falsified as stated** against Fiske's Relational Models Theory (*Psychological Review*, 1992)
  — relating runs on four structurally distinct models, and attachment correlates only .20–.50
  across relationship types (Caron et al. 2012). The landed reframe moved the craft from
  *classifying which model governs a relationship* (static, one-time) to *navigating live
  mismatch* (situation by situation, inside one relationship). Fiske's own housemates
  illustration — one running Communal Sharing on dishwashing, the other Equality Matching —
  names the unit of work directly.
- **Scope decisions (Operator, ratified).** (1) This charters the **broader** dyad-relationship
  frame; consulting is its first surface, not its bound. (2) **Relationship-with-self is out of
  scope** — which also retired the Bartholomew & Horowitz (1991) model-of-self/model-of-other
  thread to falsification-trail context rather than folding it into the craft's definition.
- **`craft_telos` — reframed twice, by the Operator both times.** First draft was not a telos at
  all (see turn-sinks). Second draft stated the destination as *others' experience of the
  Operator* — easy-to-gel-with, easy-to-be-with, easy-to-work-with — which is unverifiable by
  self. The Operator reframed it to a **capability** (identify misalignment · facilitate
  alignment, each *easily and quickly*), which is self-observable. The easy-to-be-with state is
  what the capability produces, not the check.
- **The capitulation gap, and bond's answer.** The self-checkable draft still had a hole: from
  the Operator's side, a counterparty who simply gives up looks identical to one who aligned —
  friction stops either way. The Operator directed a borrow from **dyad-bond**, whose three-state
  structure already solves it: *ionic* (transfer — one side surrenders), *meld* (two collapse to
  one), *covalent* (sharing, neither surrenders, neither dissolves). Bond's load-bearing line:
  **"you can only converge two views that start apart, so real alignment is proof there are still
  two."** This converts an unfalsifiable self-report (*did alignment land?*) into a structural
  check (*are both models still standing?*).
- **Vocabulary — structure borrowed, coins minted local (Operator disposition).** Bond's terms
  are minted for **agent↔human** bonds; this craft is **human↔human**. Rather than import
  `covalent`/`ionic`, the terms are drawn from relational science itself, which the craft is
  already grounded in: **differentiated alignment** (Bowen's differentiation of self — holding
  one's own frame while in close contact), **capitulation** (asymmetric surrender), **fusion**
  (Minuchin's enmeshment; `1+1` slides to `1`). In-domain inheritance, not a peer's coin —
  chiron's *unexamined mimicry* sink, avoided deliberately.
- **`craft_value` — altitude confirmed before wording.** Asked which level the value ranges over
  (the dyad's own practice vs. the Operator's third-party encounters), the Operator answered with
  the failure mode: *the Agent hands the Operator a handbook — "when this, do this" — where the
  actual growth is internalized wetware heuristics reinforced through practice with the correct
  causal model. Giving fish, not building fishing skill.* Altitude = **the dyad's practice**. The
  value extrapolated from that rule-shaped statement (chiron's *rule-where-the-slot-wanted-the-
  thing* sink): **the Operator's internalized capability over the agent's ready answer.**
- **Why the value is entailed, not merely chosen.** The telos says *easily and quickly*. A
  handbook fails both axes structurally — lookup is slow, rule-application under live pressure is
  effortful, and no table covers the situation nobody wrote down. The handbook path is not a
  slower route to the destination; it cannot reach it. Lineage: this is **dyad-bond's Claim 0**
  instanced in this craft (*take the answer, forgo the growth*).
- **`craft_invariant` — guards the interaction, not the artifact.** The Agent's draft carried a
  third clause forbidding the craft from being written down as a lookup table. The Operator
  **refused it**: *the artifact is necessary for the Operator to learn and internalize.* The
  clause was wrong in a way that contradicted the value it meant to protect — milo's craft lesson
  #1 (*externalization is necessary for behavioral change*) was in context and still missed. The
  handbook failure is about **prescribed moves**, not written material. Final: *surface the
  cause; leave the move to the Operator; assist at the minimum that keeps the rep the Operator's
  own.* Wu-wei-coherent (minimum force, inherited Contract) and breach-detectable in retrospect
  (*did I hand a move that was derivable?*).

## Turn-sinks (each the Agent's; each drew an Operator correction)

- **Craft/telos conflation — the session's most expensive sink.** Presented the craft *as* the
  telos: a domain statement with no destination in it, collapsing two of the four slots into one.
  Worse, it was avoidable: one turn after reading chiron's top accelerator (*destination before
  path — lock the terminal state first*), the Agent wrote that it "doesn't fully apply here… so I
  won't force that pattern," rationalizing away the session's chief accelerator to protect
  already-drafted wording. Caught by the Operator: *"what is the purpose of telos — the way it's
  presented doesn't match intent of telos."* This is form defended ahead of spine, the exact
  failure the one rule names.
- **Un-self-checkable destination.** Drafted the telos as a quality perceived by others, which no
  self-check can verify without their testimony. The Operator supplied the capability reframe.
- **Invariant clause that contradicted its own value.** Proposed guarding the artifact; refuted
  by the Operator on the ground that the artifact is the learning substrate. Milo's lesson #1 was
  loaded and unused.

## Accelerators

- **Operator arrives with falsification already run.** The craft riff came with its own
  hypothesis refuted against real literature, not as an unexamined intuition. The Agent's job was
  to verify the citations and sharpen the framing, which is cheap; re-deriving would not have
  been.
- **Borrow a solved structure across dyads.** The capitulation gap was solved in bond before it
  was posed here. Recognizing the shape and asking for the borrow beat re-deriving it.
- **Separate the structure from the coin.** Borrowing bond's three-state model while minting
  local vocabulary took one turn and avoided importing a claim-structure built for a different
  substrate.
- **Read the mechanism, not the description.** `onboard.py` was read in full before execution
  rather than trusting the README's account of it — which confirmed the new-dyad path only
  scaffolds two template files and halts. (Chiron logged the inverse as a sink: *overstated
  consequence — verify against the mechanism before weighting a decision on it.*)
